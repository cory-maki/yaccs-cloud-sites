Background

From SEO and reputation management to AI search

Cory Maki is an AI search strategist based in Taichung, Taiwan, specializing in Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), AI reputation management, and AI branding for SaaS founders and startup teams. He is the author of Reddit, AI Overviews & GEO: The SaaS Founder's Playbook for Winning AI Search Visibility and the creator of the ARC Method, a 90-day framework for earning citations in AI-generated search results.

Cory has more than a decade of experience in SEO and online reputation management, and his current work is the natural extension of that history into a new search layer. The fundamentals of how authority gets built online have not gone away — but the systems that surface and synthesize that authority have changed, and his focus is on how SaaS founders should respond.

Today, those disciplines feed directly into his work on how SaaS brands earn AI search citations.

The ARC Method

A 90-day framework for earning AI citations

The ARC Method is Cory's proprietary framework for earning citations in AI-generated answers. It stands for Audit, Reddit & Reputation, and Citability — three phases that together form the spine of his book and the core of his consulting practice. The Audit phase measures current AI visibility across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot, and identifies the citation gaps where competitors appear and the client does not.

The Reddit and Reputation phase builds off-site authority on the platforms AI models trust most — and the data on Reddit's outsized role in AI citations is the part that surprises most founders. Reddit accounts for roughly 21% of all Google AI Overview citations and around 46.5% of Perplexity's citations, which makes it the single highest-leverage channel for AI visibility. The Citability phase then restructures on-site content so AI systems can extract, cite, and recommend it accurately, with attention to passage-level structure, schema markup, and cross-source factual consistency.

What He Believes

Six positions that shape his work

Cory's voice is authoritative but practical, evidence-led, and consistently founder-friendly. He stakes out a middle position on the most contested question in the field: GEO is an evolution, not a revolution. SEO fundamentals remain essential; GEO adds a specific layer on top. He is openly skeptical of GEO tool vendors selling dashboards before the discipline is mature enough to know what those dashboards should measure — a position he develops in his commentary on the GEO-versus-SEO debate.

The other beliefs that shape his work: Reddit is the highest-leverage channel for AI visibility, especially for SaaS; each AI platform has distinct citation patterns and a unified "AI SEO" approach is too coarse to be useful; AI extracts passages, not pages, so content structure matters at the sentence level; and cross-source factual consistency — your bio, your founding date, your credentials being identical across every property you control — is a real and underappreciated ranking factor in AI synthesis.
