What I Do

Helping SaaS brands get cited by AI search engines.

I’m an AI search strategist. My work is helping SaaS founders and startup teams earn citations in the AI-generated answers buyers now see before they click anything — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot. The economics are stark: when an AI Overview appears, only about 8% of users click a traditional result, and the answer itself names just three or four brands. Out of 18 million domains, only 274,000 have ever appeared in an AI Overview at all.

Most SaaS companies have decent SEO foundations and no strategy at all for the AI layer. That’s the gap I work in. I run AI visibility audits, build out Reddit and reputation strategies, restructure on-site content for citability, and walk founders through the 90-day ARC implementation. The signature framework — Audit, Reddit & Reputation, Citability — is the spine of the book and the consulting work alike.

Signature Framework

The ARC Method.

ARC stands for Audit, Reddit & Reputation, and Citability — the three phases of a 90-day program I developed after watching dozens of SaaS clients realize their competitors were showing up in ChatGPT answers and they weren’t. Audit measures current visibility across each AI platform and identifies the citation gaps. Reddit & Reputation builds off-site authority on the platforms AI models trust most — Reddit alone accounts for roughly 21% of Google AI Overview citations and 46.5% of Perplexity’s. Citability restructures on-site content so AI systems can extract, cite, and recommend it accurately.

ARC isn’t a replacement for SEO. It assumes you already have the fundamentals and adds the layer that now sits on top of them. The full playbook — including subreddit scoring, comment frameworks, schema guidance, and a week-by-week plan — is in the book.

The Book

Reddit, AI Overviews & GEO.

Reddit, AI Overviews & GEO: The SaaS Founder’s Playbook for Winning AI Search Visibility came out on April 6, 2026 (Kindle, ASIN B0GGXGT2GD).
