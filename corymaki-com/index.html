About

From SEO veteran to AI search strategist.

I spent more than a decade running SEO and online reputation campaigns — for SaaS startups, law firms, and high-net-worth individuals — long before AI Overviews started rewriting the rules of search. The fundamentals didn’t go away. A whole layer got added on top of them.

Today I focus almost entirely on that new layer: Generative Engine Optimization. AI Overviews now appear in over half of U.S. searches, only about 8% of users click an organic result when they do, and the answers themselves cite just 3–4 brands at a time. The work is figuring out how to be one of them — and the ARC Method is the 90-day framework I built to get founders there.

I’m based in Taichung, Taiwan, where I run Digital Forge alongside the consulting and writing work. My book — Reddit, AI Overviews & GEO — distills everything I’ve learned about how AI models choose the brands they cite. The podcast, Search Signals, is where I work through what each platform is actually doing in real time.
